# Acceptable use and data handling

## Acceptable use of company devices

Company laptops, accounts, and network are provided for work. Limited personal use is fine as long as it is legal, does not interfere with work, and does not put company data or systems at risk. Do not install unlicensed software or disable security tools.

## Data classification levels

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Public can be shared freely. Internal is the default for everyday work and stays within the company. Confidential is limited to people with a need to know. Restricted covers the most sensitive data and has extra handling rules.

## Sharing files safely

Share files through company drives and links with access limited to the people who need them, not by emailing copies around. Never put Confidential or Restricted data into personal cloud accounts, personal email, or public AI tools.

## Using AI assistants with company data

You may use approved AI tools for Internal and Public information. Do not paste Confidential or Restricted data, secrets, credentials, or personal data about others into any external AI service. This internal support assistant is approved for Internal information.

## Personal devices

You may read email and chat on an enrolled personal phone through the official apps. Personal computers may not join the VPN or store company files; use the browser remote portal for occasional access instead.

## Leaving the company

On your last day, return all equipment and access ends automatically. Copying company data for personal use before leaving is not permitted.
