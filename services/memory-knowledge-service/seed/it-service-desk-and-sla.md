# Contacting the service desk and how priorities work

## How to reach the service desk

You can reach IT support through this support assistant, the web portal, email, or the phone line for urgent issues. The assistant and portal are fastest for routine requests because they route straight to the right team without a callback.

## What information to have ready

Have ready a clear description of the problem, when it started, any error messages word for word, what you already tried, and whether it blocks your work. For a device issue, include the asset tag from the sticker on the laptop.

## Priority levels

Priority is set from impact and urgency. Critical means a whole team or a business‑critical service is down. High means one person is fully blocked. Medium means you can work with a workaround. Low means a question or a minor annoyance.

## Response and resolution targets

Target first response is 15 minutes for Critical, 1 hour for High, 4 business hours for Medium, and 1 business day for Low. Resolution targets are longer and depend on the fix. You will be told if a ticket needs parts, a vendor, or a change window.

## Following up on an existing ticket

Reply to the ticket's email thread or open it in the portal and add a comment; both reach the assigned agent. Avoid opening a second ticket for the same issue, as it splits the history and slows things down.

## After-hours and emergencies

Outside business hours, only Critical issues get an immediate response through the phone line. Non‑urgent requests submitted after hours are picked up the next business morning in priority order.
