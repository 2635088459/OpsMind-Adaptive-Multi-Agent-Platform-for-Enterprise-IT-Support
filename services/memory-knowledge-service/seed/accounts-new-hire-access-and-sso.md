# New-hire access and SSO

## What access a new hire gets automatically

On your start date you automatically get an account, email, chat, VPN eligibility, and the standard software set for your department. Anything beyond that baseline, including the Housing Portal and finance or admin tools, must be requested and approved by your manager.

## Requesting additional application access

Use the access request form, choose the application, and state your business reason. Your manager and the application owner both approve. Most requests complete within one business day; provisioning for some systems runs on an hourly schedule after approval.

## SSO error "no access to this application"

This message means single sign‑on worked but you have not been granted a role in that specific application. Submit an access request for it. Signing in again will not help until the grant is in place.

## SSO error "authentication failed" across all apps

If single sign‑on fails for every application, the problem is your account or password, not any one app. Do a self‑service password reset and make sure a multi‑factor method is enrolled, then try again.

## Access after changing teams

Changing teams does not automatically remove old access or add new access. Ask your new manager to submit access requests for what you need now, and tell the service desk which old systems you no longer use so that access can be removed.
