# Password reset

## Reset your own password

You can reset your password yourself at any time from the identity portal by choosing "Forgot password" and confirming a multi‑factor prompt on your enrolled device. The new password takes effect everywhere within a few minutes. There is no need to open a ticket for a routine password reset.

## Password reset link by email

If the support assistant offers to send a password‑reset link to your registered email, accept it and follow the link within 15 minutes. The link is single‑use. If it expires, request a new one.

## Password rules

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords. A passphrase of a few unrelated words is easier to remember and meets the rule. Passwords expire every 180 days.

## Password changed but old one still works somewhere

After a password change, apps that cached the old password (often Outlook, the VPN client, or phone mail) will prompt again. Enter the new password once in each. If a device is not prompting, sign out of the account on that device and back in.

## You cannot reset because MFA is unavailable

Self‑service reset needs a working multi‑factor method. If you have lost access to all of your methods, contact the service desk to verify your identity in person or by video and issue a temporary code.
