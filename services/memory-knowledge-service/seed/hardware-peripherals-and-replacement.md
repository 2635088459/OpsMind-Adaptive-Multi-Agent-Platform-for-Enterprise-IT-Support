# Peripherals and equipment replacement

## External monitor not detected

If an external monitor is not detected, reseat both ends of the video cable, try a different cable, and check the monitor's input source setting. On a dock, unplug the dock from the laptop for 10 seconds and reconnect. Update pending system updates, which often include display and dock firmware.

## Docking station not working

A dock that stops passing through power, network, or USB usually needs a full power cycle: unplug the dock from wall power and from the laptop, wait 30 seconds, then reconnect wall power first and the laptop last. If one port type never works, the dock may be faulty and should be swapped.

## Keyboard, mouse, or headset not working

For a wired device, try another USB port and another machine to isolate the fault. For a wireless device, replace the batteries and re‑pair it. Company‑standard peripherals that are genuinely dead are replaced same‑week through the service desk at no cost to your team.

## Requesting a replacement or additional peripheral

Request replacement or additional standard peripherals such as a keyboard, mouse, headset, webcam, or a second monitor through the service desk. Standard items are pre‑approved. Non‑standard or premium items need manager approval and a business reason.

## Returning equipment when leaving or changing roles

When you leave the company or no longer need a device, return it to the service desk in person or by the provided shipping label. Do not pass company equipment directly to a coworker; it has to be wiped and re‑issued.
