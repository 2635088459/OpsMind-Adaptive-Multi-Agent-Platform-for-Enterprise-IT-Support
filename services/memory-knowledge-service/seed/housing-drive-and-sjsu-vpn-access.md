# Housing Drive access and SJSU VPN

## Cannot access the Housing public Drive

If you cannot access or open the Housing public Drive, you are almost always not connected to the VPN. To get access to the public Drive, connect to the campus VPN at one.sjsu.edu and sign in with your school account, then open the Drive link again. The public Drive is only reachable from the campus network or the VPN, so drive access will keep failing on a plain home or guest connection until the VPN is connected.

## VPN shows a permission or access-denied error

If connecting to the VPN returns an error that says you do not have permission or access, it means your account has not been granted VPN access yet rather than a network fault. Submit a ticket or contact the Housing IT team to request VPN access. Retrying the connection will not help until the access is granted.

## Signing in to a school computer after a recent password change

If you have changed the password for your school account recently, you must sign in to the school computer with the new password, not the old one. The computer does not update on its own. Using the old password will fail to log in and can lock the account after repeated attempts, so update any saved password on that machine as well.

## Requesting access for a new Housing employee

To request access for a new Housing employee, contact the Housing IT team and fill out the access request form. The form must carry the required signatures from each level of leadership before it can be processed. Submit it with all approvals in place; an incomplete or unsigned form will be sent back and delays provisioning.
