# Mailbox is full or over quota

## What "mailbox full" means

When your mailbox reaches its storage quota you stop being able to send mail, and eventually stop receiving it. The standard mailbox quota is 50 GB. You will get a warning email at 45 GB. Clearing space restores sending within a few minutes.

## Fastest way to free mailbox space

Sort your mail by size and delete or archive the largest items, which are almost always messages with big attachments. Empty the Deleted Items folder afterwards, because items there still count against your quota until purged.

## Using online archive for old mail

Ask the service desk to enable your Online Archive if you need to keep old mail. Once enabled, mail older than one year moves automatically to the archive, which has its own separate large quota and stays searchable in Outlook.

## Large attachments should not go by email

Do not email attachments larger than 25 MB; they will be rejected. Upload the file to your team drive or file share and send the link instead. This also keeps everyone's mailbox smaller.

## Shared mailbox is full

A shared mailbox has its own quota separate from your personal one. If a shared mailbox is full, every delegate is blocked from sending as it. The mailbox owner should clear space or request a quota increase from the service desk.
