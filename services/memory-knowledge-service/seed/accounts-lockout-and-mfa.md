# Account lockout and MFA device changes

## Account is locked

Your account locks after several failed sign‑ins and unlocks automatically after 15 minutes. If you need in sooner, complete a self‑service password reset, which also clears the lock. Repeated lockouts usually mean a device still has an old cached password.

## Unlock is not working

If the account is still locked after 15 minutes and a password reset does not clear it, an administrator hold may be in place, often for a security review. Contact the service desk; they can see the hold reason and release it if appropriate.

## Enrolling a new MFA device

Add a new multi‑factor device from the identity portal under Security before you lose the old one. Keep at least two methods enrolled, for example an authenticator app and a hardware key or backup codes, so losing one device never locks you out.

## Replacing a lost or stolen MFA device

If your only MFA device is lost or stolen, contact the service desk immediately so the old device can be removed from your account and a temporary code issued. Then enroll the replacement right away and delete the temporary method.

## Getting a new phone

Before wiping or trading in an old phone, enroll the authenticator on the new phone and confirm it works. Moving to a new phone without re‑enrolling will block every multi‑factor sign‑in until the service desk helps you recover.
