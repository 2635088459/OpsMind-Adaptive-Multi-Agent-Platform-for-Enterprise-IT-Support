# Printing troubleshooting

## Printer will not print

If a printer will not print, first make sure your computer is connected by wired Ethernet, not wifi. Most office printers are only reachable over the wired network, so a print job sent from a wifi connection sits in the queue and never comes out. Plug in the Ethernet cable or your dock, then send the print job again.

## Printing while on wifi requires the VPN

If you can only use wifi and cannot plug in Ethernet, you must connect to the VPN before you print. The VPN puts your computer back on the office network so the printer becomes reachable. Connect the VPN first, then print. Printing over wifi without the VPN will not work.

## Make sure the correct printer is selected

A print job that seems to go through but produces no paper is usually going to the wrong printer. In the print dialog, check that the selected printer matches the physical machine you are standing at. Confirm the printer title, because several printers often have similar names, and pick the one whose title matches the label on the machine.
