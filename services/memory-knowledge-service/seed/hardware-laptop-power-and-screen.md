# Laptop power and screen problems

## Laptop will not turn on

If the laptop will not turn on at all, connect the official charger and leave it for at least 15 minutes, then try again. Do a forced power cycle by holding the power button for 10 seconds. If there is still no light, no fan, and no screen, the device needs hardware service; contact the service desk to arrange a repair or a loaner.

## Laptop screen is cracked or the display is broken

A cracked screen, bleeding colors, flickering lines, or a display that stays black while the laptop is clearly running are physical hardware faults. These cannot be fixed remotely. Report it to the service desk for an in‑person repair or replacement, and stop using the device if glass is loose.

## Laptop is very slow or overheating

A laptop that is slow and hot usually has too many applications open or a stuck background process. Restart it, close apps you are not using, and make sure the vents are not blocked by a soft surface. If it is still slow after a restart with little running, open a ticket.

## Battery drains fast or will not charge

If the battery drains unusually fast or will not charge past a low percentage, note the health figure from the battery settings screen and report it. Batteries are considered consumable and are replaced through the service desk when health drops below 70 percent.

## Laptop is wet or was dropped

If the laptop got wet or took a hard fall, power it off, do not charge it, and contact the service desk. Continuing to use a liquid‑damaged device can turn a small problem into a total loss.
