# VPN connection troubleshooting

## VPN will not connect at all

If the VPN client will not connect, first confirm your internet works without the VPN by loading any public website. Then fully quit the VPN client (not just close the window) and reopen it. If it still will not connect, write down the exact error code shown and contact the service desk with that code.

## VPN connects then drops after a few minutes

A VPN session that connects and then drops repeatedly is almost always an unstable local network. Switch from Wi‑Fi to a wired connection if you can, move closer to the access point, or try a different network such as a phone hotspot. If the drops only happen on one specific network, report that network name to the service desk.

## VPN error "authentication failed"

An "authentication failed" message when starting the VPN usually means your password recently changed or your multi‑factor device is not enrolled. Sign in to the identity portal in a browser first to confirm your credentials work, complete any pending multi‑factor prompts, then retry the VPN.

## VPN is connected but internal sites do not load

If the VPN shows connected but internal applications such as the Housing Portal still do not load, disconnect and reconnect the VPN once to refresh routing. If that does not help, restart your machine while on the corporate network. Persistent failures should go to the service desk with the name of the internal site that fails.

## Which VPN profile to use

Use the "Corporate-Full" VPN profile for normal work. The "Corporate-Split" profile only routes internal traffic and should be used only when the service desk tells you to for a specific troubleshooting step.
