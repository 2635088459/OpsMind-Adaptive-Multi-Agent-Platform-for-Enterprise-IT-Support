# Phishing and spam

## How to report a suspected phishing email

Use the "Report Phishing" button in the Outlook toolbar. This sends the message to the security team and removes it from your inbox. Do not forward the suspicious message to colleagues, and do not click any links or open attachments in it.

## I already clicked a link in a phishing email

If you clicked a link or entered your password on a suspicious page, change your password immediately from a known‑good device and then contact the service desk right away. Speed matters; the sooner it is reported, the sooner access can be protected.

## Legitimate email went to Junk

If a real message landed in the Junk Email folder, right‑click it and choose "Not Junk", then add the sender to your Safe Senders list. If a whole trusted domain keeps getting filtered, ask the service desk to allow that domain centrally.

## Too much spam getting through

If spam is getting past the filter, report a few examples with the "Report Junk" button so the filter learns. Never reply to spam or use its unsubscribe link, as that confirms your address is active.

## Suspicious internal message from a coworker

If a message appears to come from a coworker but asks for gift cards, urgent payments, or your password, treat it as an attack even if the name looks right. Verify with that person through chat or in person, and report the email as phishing.
