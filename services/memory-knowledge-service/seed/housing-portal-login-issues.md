# Housing Portal login issues

## Cannot sign in to the Housing Portal

The Housing Portal uses your normal company single sign‑on. If you cannot sign in, first confirm you can sign in to another SSO app such as email. If email works but the Housing Portal does not, clear your browser cache for the portal site or try a private browsing window.

## Housing Portal says "account not provisioned"

An "account not provisioned" message means your profile has not been created in the Housing Portal yet. This is normal on your first day or if your role recently changed. Request Housing Portal access through the access request form; provisioning runs hourly.

## Redirect loop when opening the Housing Portal

If the Housing Portal keeps bouncing you back to the login page, your session cookie is stale. Sign out fully, close all browser tabs for the site, delete cookies for the portal domain, then open it fresh. Corporate‑managed browsers are required; the portal blocks unmanaged browsers.

## Housing Portal login works but the page is blank

A blank page after login is usually a browser extension or an ad blocker interfering. Disable extensions for the portal domain, or use the company standard browser profile with no added extensions.

## Multi-factor prompt not appearing for the Housing Portal

The Housing Portal relies on the same multi‑factor method as the rest of SSO. If no prompt appears, open your authenticator app directly and enter the code. If you have no enrolled method, enroll one in the identity portal first.
