# Shared mailboxes and distribution lists

## Requesting access to a shared mailbox

To get access to a shared mailbox, the mailbox owner must approve your request. Submit a request naming the shared mailbox address and the access level you need: "send as", "send on behalf", or read‑only. Access is usually granted within one business day.

## A shared mailbox does not appear in Outlook

After you are granted access, a shared mailbox can take up to an hour to appear and sometimes needs Outlook restarted. If it still does not show up, add it manually through Account Settings, More Settings, Advanced, Add mailbox, and type its address.

## Creating a new distribution list

Request a new distribution list by giving a proposed list name, its purpose, an owner, and the initial members. Distribution lists are for email only; if you need shared files or chat, request a team workspace instead.

## Adding or removing distribution list members

The listed owner of a distribution list can add and remove members themselves from the address book properties. If you are not the owner, send your change to the owner or to the service desk with the owner copied for approval.

## Emails to a distribution list bounce back

If mail to a distribution list bounces, the list may be restricted to internal senders or may have been retired. Check with the list owner. External senders usually need to be explicitly allowed on the list.
